Steak Tacos

Explination

Steak tacos are another staple of the hispanic and latin food, there most popular in mexico, but still central and south americas still eat this delishus food.

tips on making Seak tacos

  • Slice the steak against the grain so every bite stays tender.
  • Let the meat rest for five minutes after cooking so the juices stay put.
  • Warm the tortillas on a dry pan until they puff slightly.
  • No skirt steak? Flank steak or sirloin works well.
  • Swap the lime marinade for orange juice if you want it a little sweeter.

The Recipe

Total preparation time: 45 minutes (plus 30 minutes to marinate)

Servings: 4 people (about 12 tacos)

Ingredients
IngredientAmount
Skirt steak1.5 lbs
Lime juice3 tbsp
Garlic, minced3 cloves
Cumin1 tsp
Salt1 tsp
Corn tortillas12
White onion, diced1/2 cup
Fresh cilantro, chopped1/2 cup
Salsa and lime wedgesTo serve
Instruction
StepWhat to do
1Combine lime juice, garlic, cumin, and salt. Coat the steak and marinate for 30 minutes.
2Heat a skillet or grill over high heat.
3Cook the steak 3-4 minutes per side for medium.
4Rest the steak 5 minutes, then slice thinly against the grain.
5Warm the tortillas on a dry pan about 30 seconds per side.
6Fill the tortillas with steak, onion, and cilantro. Serve with salsa and lime.
