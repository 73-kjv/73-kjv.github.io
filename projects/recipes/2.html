Explanation

Papusas is a hispanicn food that is loved in the north, centeral, and south americas, in the food they mostly stuff them with cheass and beans. but some put different kinds of ingredients, is is the staple of hispanic food

Tips for making it

  • Keep a bowl of water nearby and wet your hands so the masa does not stick.
  • If the masa cracks at the edges, knead in a spoonful of warm water.
  • Quesillo is traditional, but mozzarella melts nicely too.
  • Refried black beans, or loroco are great fillings.

The Recipe

Total preparation time: 50 minutes

Servings: 4 people (8 papusas)

Ingredients
IngredientAmount
Masa harina2 cups
Warm water1 1/2 cups
Salt1/2 tsp
Shredded quesillo or mozzarella2 cups
Refried beans1 cup
Vegetable oil1 tbsp, for the pan
Curtido and salsaTo serve
Instructions
StepWhat to do
1Mix masa harina, salt, and warm water into a soft dough. Rest 10 minutes.
2Divide the dough into 8 balls.
3With wet hands, flatten a ball into a cup. Add a spoonful each of cheese and beans.
4Close the dough around the filling and pat it into a disc about 1/2 inch thick.
5Cook on a lightly oiled griddle over medium heat, 4-5 minutes per side, until golden.
6Serve hot with curtido and salsa.
